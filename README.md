# ⭐ Featured Project: Workforce Attrition Analytics

### PostgreSQL | Python | Pandas | Tableau | Workforce Analytics

An end-to-end workforce analytics project examining employee attrition across **overtime, job role, compensation, tenure, and job satisfaction**.

The project combines SQL analysis in PostgreSQL, exploratory analysis and feature engineering in Python, and an interactive Tableau dashboard to identify workforce segments associated with elevated turnover.

### Key Findings

- **16.1%** overall employee attrition rate
- Employees working overtime had **30.5% attrition**, compared with **10.4%** among employees not working overtime
- **Sales Representatives had the highest job-role attrition rate at 39.8%**
- Sales Representatives working overtime experienced **66.7% attrition**
- Employees with less than one year of tenure had **36.4% attrition**
- Employees reporting the lowest job satisfaction had **22.8% attrition**, compared with **11.3%** among employees reporting the highest satisfaction
